What We Do

Complete Thatch Roofing Services

Everything your thatch roof needs — under one expert roof. Repairs, new builds, protection and conversions, all done to the highest standard.

Thatch Roofs & Repairs

Leaks, storm damage or worn patches — we repair thatch roofs fast and properly, restoring them to full strength.

New Thatch Roofs

Building from scratch? We craft brand-new thatch roofs for homes, lapas and lodges — designed to impress and endure.

Re-Thatching

Give a tired roof a complete new life. Full re-thatching that looks brand new and protects for decades to come.

Re-Brushing

A professional re-brush refreshes your thatch surface, clears debris and extends the lifespan of your existing roof.

Overhaul & Extensions

Expanding your space? We overhaul existing structures and add seamless thatch extensions that blend perfectly.

Fire Retardant & Fireproofing

Protect your investment with professional fire retardant treatment — added peace of mind for every thatch roof.

Lightning Conductors

KZN storms are no joke. We install proper lightning conductors to safeguard your roof, home and family.

Fumigation & Insect Control

Keep borers, insects and pests out of your thatch with thorough fumigation and ongoing insect control treatments.

Harvey Tile Conversion

Want a lower-maintenance roof? We expertly convert thatch to durable Harvey tiles — clean, modern and long-lasting.
